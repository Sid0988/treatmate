Born from Love, Backed by Science

Like many pet parents, we searched tirelessly for treats that weren't packed with hidden fillers, artificial flavors, or wheat gluten. We wanted a reward that did more than just taste good—we wanted real functional nutrition.

That’s how TreatMate was born. Inspired by ancient Himalayan yak cheese traditions, we perfected recipes that combine high-protein yak milk, soothing goat milk, and superfoods like blueberries, pumpkin, and seaweed.
